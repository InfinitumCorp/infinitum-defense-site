Questions
What is surveillance detection?

Watching for people who are watching you. Most attacks are preceded by reconnaissance, and spotting it gives you time to act.

Can you staff our security operations center?

Yes. Command post staffing covers monitoring, coordination and escalation from your control room.

How do you keep long-term posts sharp?

With written post orders, patrol discipline and regular review of the plan as conditions at the site change.
