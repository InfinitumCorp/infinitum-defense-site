Questions
What is the difference between executive protection and a bodyguard?

A bodyguard reacts. Executive protection plans: routes, venues, schedules and threat intelligence are worked out in advance so most problems never reach the principal.

Can you protect executives when they travel?

Yes. Travel and movement planning is part of the service, and it pairs with our secure transport and protective intelligence teams.

Will protection disrupt the principal's work?

It shouldn't. We plan around the principal's schedule and keep the detail as discreet as the risk allows.
