About Infinitum Defense

Infinitum Defense is the defensive arm of Infinitum Corporation. We operate physically, on the ground, in denied areas where access is restricted and the risk is real.

Infinitum Corporation provides the technology, intelligence and strategy. Infinitum Defense puts trained people on site to protect them. Visit Infinitum Corporation ↗

Consulting

International private security consulting

Based in Washington, D.C., we deliver security services that protect property and businesses for one day or indefinitely. We sit down with owners and leadership to build a comprehensive security plan that fits how the organization actually operates.
